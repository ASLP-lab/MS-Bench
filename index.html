01 / Motivation

Aggregate accuracy hides where systems break.

Two systems can have similar WER while failing for entirely different reasons. MS-Bench separates recognition, segmentation, and speaker attribution, then measures each under controlled conversational conditions.

Input condition
Concurrent speechWho spoke what, and when?
→
LexicalWhat was said?Substitutions, insertions, deletions
SegmentationWas speech found?Missed or fabricated regions
AttributionWho said it?Words assigned to the wrong speaker

02 / Benchmark

Five axes.
A fuller stress test.

Each recording receives a compact condition profile. Stratification turns a diverse collection into an interpretable diagnostic space.

01

Speaker count

From dyadic speech to conversations with fourteen speakers.

2 → 14 speakers
02

Overlap ratio

Measures how much concurrent speech challenges recognition and attribution.

none → severe
03

Speaker similarity

Uses the most confusable voice pair within each recording.

distinct → similar
04

Turn interval

Characterizes rapid transitions without conflating concurrent speech.

rapid → relaxed
05

Acoustic quality

Combines DNSMOS and NISQA ranks into one degradation measure.

clean → degraded
